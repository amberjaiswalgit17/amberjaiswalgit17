## ⚙️ Engineering Philosophy

> **Code is the implementation. Engineering is everything that happens around it.**

> **Engineering is the discipline of making future problems smaller.**

> **Build with intention. Simplify with experience.**

I believe good engineering is not just about making software work.

It is about understanding the problem deeply, choosing the right abstractions,
anticipating failure, reducing complexity, and leaving the system easier to
understand and maintain than before.

### Principles I Work By

- **Ownership over task completion**
- **Clarity over cleverness**
- **Simple systems over unnecessary complexity**
- **Correctness before optimization**
- **Design for change, not just today's requirements**
- **Measure before optimizing**
- **Document decisions, not just code**
- **Build systems that future engineers can trust**
